About Me

Jaylah Williams is a professional dancer.she attends Laguardia Highschool as a dance major wheres shes getting a vibrant amount of training. As shes currently working on her junior projects. She has been dancing since the age of 3. She started dancing at Mark Morris for a few years. She also took dance classes in new york as an training method. By the age of 7 she began to dance with this dance company called Biancas Dance Acacdemy where she learned all genres of dance that consist of jazz, tap, ballet, hiphop, afro-caribean, and contemporary. At this company she was able to do many dance competitions worldwide , where she won plenty of trophies, awards, opportunites, and even the highest score in her solo out of the whole competition.

After years with this company, she moved on and continued to take different dance classes in new york. After trying out for the Brooklyn Nets Kids twice, her third try she made the team. Her 3 years of being a Brooklyn Nets Kids and Lil Torch of the New York Liberty basketball team was such a great way of gaining opportunies and truly training in the professional world where she also was captain her last season.

After her Nets Kids years, she auditioned for Harlem School Of The Arts, where she got in with a scholarship for the summer intensive and year round program. At HSA they give plenty of opportunies and training techniques hat constant of all genres of dance. At HSA she has been given the opportunities to perform at plenty of dance shows and perfomances. She was even able to to open for the Radio City Rockettes.
