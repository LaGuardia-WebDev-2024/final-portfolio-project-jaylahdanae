About Me

Jaylah Williams is a professional dancer. She has been dancing since the age of 3. She started dancing at Mark Morris for a few years. By the age of 7 she began to dance with this dance company called Biancas Dance Acacdemy where she learned all genres of dance that consist of jazz, tap, ballet, hiphop, afro-caribean, and contemporary.
